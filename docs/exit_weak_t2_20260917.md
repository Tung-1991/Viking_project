# Exit research — E 3/6 + weak exit after T+2 (2026-09-17)

## Scope and method

- Seven independent REPLAY cohorts: CTS, FTS, SHS, SSI, VIX, VND, DPM. Each symbol starts with VND 1 billion, maximum one position, 60% exposure, no compounding. These totals are **sums of independent symbol tests**, not a single shared-capital portfolio.
- Entry remains after 14:00 on EMA 3/6 + RSI. Baseline exit is SL (-4% initial, -2.1% re-entry), E 3/6 and AUTO PROTECT (dynamic ON; START ATR14(T-1) × 0.6; TRAIL ATR × 0.8; retention 87.5% below 5% MFE; ARM 7%, giveback 2.5%; sell 100%). T+2 RECHECK is used.
- MFE comparison is the **net after-T+2 sellable-phase MFE while each position is held**, with simulated fees/tax. Each position appears once in this denominator; pre-T+2 MFE is separately diagnostic and is never added. PnL includes closed trades and end-of-window open-position mark-to-market where applicable.
- Candidate keeps the baseline exits and adds one optional E condition: once the **entire position is sellable**, gross unrealized return ≤ -1%, EMA 2 < EMA 4 and current daily RSI < previous daily RSI, sell 100%. SL and TP retain priority. This is an early-loss branch, not a change to ordinary E 3/6.

## Same-data replay comparison

| Variant | Net PnL (VND m) | Sellable MFE (VND m) | PnL/MFE | Closed / open | Worst per-symbol max DD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline E 3/6 | 568.82 | 913.84 | 62.25% | 56 / 1 | 7.43% |
| Weak exit ≤ -0.5% | 585.40 | 908.32 | 64.45% | 57 / 0 | — |
| Weak exit ≤ -1.0% | **599.53** | **915.77** | **65.47%** | **57 / 0** | **7.09%** |
| Weak exit ≤ -1.5% | 597.78 | 915.77 | 65.28% | 57 / 0 | — |

At -1%, the PnL gain over E 3/6 is VND 30.71m and sellable MFE gain is VND 1.93m. All 57 BUY fills match the baseline in timestamp and price, so this gain is not from different entries. Candidate exits: 43 PROTECT, 12 E (10 from the weak branch), 2 SL. The E exit subgroup is **not** less negative overall: -136.85m versus baseline -134.27m. Total gain comes from changed exit paths, including one fewer SL and no underwater open position; do not claim this branch has solved E losses individually.

## Decision and limits

Keep `sellable_weak_exit_enabled=False` by default for live/PAPER. The candidate is available for controlled research at `sellable_weak_exit_loss_pct=-1.0`, but **65.47% is below the 70–75% target**, and the threshold was selected on the same seven cohorts. There is no untouched out-of-sample validation or live slippage/liquidity test. The goal is not guaranteed; next validation should use unseen dates/symbols, realistic execution sensitivity and then PAPER observation before enabling real orders.

The current same-cache replay baseline (568.82m) differs from an older archived 563.37m result. This note compares variants re-run under one code/data state; the older workbook is not used as a like-for-like control. The exact source of the older discrepancy is not established.

Reproduce from the repository root:

```powershell
ckvnvenv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.research_e_ema --sell-ema 3/6 --weak-loss -1 --compare
```
